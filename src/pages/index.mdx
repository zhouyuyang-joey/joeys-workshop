## Some useful links:
- [London Live Coding? Luma Calender](https://luma.com/londonlivecoding): keep track of live coding events in London
- [pastagang](https://www.pastagang.cc/): a creative group that anyone can join
- [nudel](https://nudel.cc/): the jamming room of pastagang
- [Live Coding: A User's Manual](https://livecodingbook.toplap.org/): the first comprehensive introduction to the practice and a broader cultural commentary on the potential for live coding to open up deeper questions about contemporary cultural production and computational culture.
